I am a Master of Public Health candidate at Columbia University, concentrating in Sociomedical Sciences with a focus on Sexuality, Sexual, and Reproductive Health. My work sits at the intersection of global health equity, maternal and child health, and community-centered research.

With a background in both qualitative and quantitative methods, I have contributed to research spanning ethnic health disparities in Sub-Saharan Africa, neonatal fatality prevention, and trainee preparedness for global health work in conflict settings. I am equally committed to translating research into action — whether through donor communications, stakeholder engagement, or community outreach.

I graduated Summa Cum Laude from Arizona State University with a B.A. in Global Health and a Minor in Biological Sciences, and bring B1-level French proficiency alongside experience studying abroad in Paris.
